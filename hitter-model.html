Method

Each metric is converted to a percentile among pool hitters (lower-is-better metrics such as whiff and chase are inverted). A component is the average of its available metric percentiles. The overall score is the weighted average of the components a hitter has data for, and coverage shows how much of the total weight that represents. A score built on 40% coverage is a lead, not a verdict.

Without Savant data, the model runs on Stats API proxies only: strikeout rate for contact, isolated power for damage, walk rate for decisions, and stolen base success for baserunning. Import the Savant files to replace the proxies with the real skill measures.

Validate the weights before trusting them: score past postseason rosters and check whether the model separates the lineups that produced runs in October better than regular-season wRC+ does.
